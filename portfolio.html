Portfolio Project Proposal

Purpose

The purpose of my website is to showcase my web development projects and skills so I can secure an entry-level position in the field.

Target Audience

The target audience for my portfolia include recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients who want to quickly see my work, my skills, and how to contact me.

Research: Essential Portfolio Components

  • Projects: Employers want proof of real work, not just a list of skills.
  • Skills: Lets recruiters quickly match my abilities to a job posting.
  • Resume: Gives hiring managers a familiar, downloadable summary of my experience.
  • About Me: Shows personality and communication skills.
  • Contact: Makes it easy for employers to reach me.
